Set up in a minute

  1. Install the Mac app

    It lives in the menu bar. No Dock icon, no window in your way.

  2. Choose Pair Phone…

    The Mac shows a QR code that holds your pairing and your encryption key.

  3. Scan it on Android

    Open ClipSync on your phone, scan the code, and you're syncing.

ClipSync isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it.

  1. Unzip the download and move ClipSync to Applications.
  2. Right-click ClipSync and choose Open, then Open again.

Questions

Is it secure?

Each clip is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it's uploaded. The key is created when you pair and travels only inside the QR code, from your Mac to your phone. Our server stores ciphertext it can't read, and deletes it after 24 hours.

Which Android versions work?

Android 8 and later. Automatic phone-to-Mac sending set up from the phone itself needs Android 11 or later. On older phones you can grant it once from a computer, or send by hand with the notification button, tile or share sheet.

Why does auto-send need Wireless debugging?

Since Android 10, apps in the background aren't allowed to read the clipboard. ClipSync works around this with a permission Android only hands out through its developer tools. Wireless debugging lets the app grant it to itself once, on your phone, without a computer. You can turn Wireless debugging off again afterwards.

What happens after the trial?

After 3 days your devices stay paired and your history stays on the Mac, but new clips stop syncing until you enter a license key in the app.

Can I use several phones?

Yes. One license covers your whole pairing, which is all the devices you've paired together, up to 20.
