Set up in a minute

  1. Install ClipSync on your computer

    On the Mac it lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon or window in your way.

  2. Choose Pair Phone…

    Your computer shows a QR code that holds your pairing and your encryption key.

  3. Scan it on Android

    Open ClipSync on your phone, scan the code, and you're syncing.

Opening it the first time

ClipSync isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it.

  1. Unzip the download and move ClipSync to Applications.
  2. Double-click ClipSync. macOS says it can't check it: click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “ClipSync was blocked”, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password. You only do this once.
  4. On macOS 14 you can instead right-click ClipSync, choose Open, then Open again.

Questions

Is it secure?

Each clip is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it's uploaded. The key is created when you pair and travels only inside the QR code, from your computer to your phone. Our server stores ciphertext it can't read, and deletes it after 24 hours.

Which Android versions work?

Android 8 and later. Automatic phone-to-computer sending set up from the phone itself needs Android 11 or later. On older phones you can grant it once from a computer, or send by hand with the notification button, tile or share sheet.

Why does auto-send need Wireless debugging?

Since Android 10, apps in the background aren't allowed to read the clipboard. ClipSync works around this with a permission Android only hands out through its developer tools. Wireless debugging lets the app grant it to itself once, on your phone, without a computer. You can turn Wireless debugging off again afterwards.

What happens after the trial?

After 7 days your devices stay paired and your history stays on the Mac, but new clips stop syncing until you enter a license key in the app. The trial covers up to 2 devices.

What happens when my license ends?

The app shows when your license ends. Renew before then and the new period is added on top, so you don't lose any days. If it runs out, your devices stay paired but new clips stop syncing until you renew.
