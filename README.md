# ClipSync

Copy on your Mac or Windows PC and paste on your Android phone, and back. Text and images, end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM; the key never leaves your devices).

**Website:** https://cyberwizard-dev.github.io/clipsync-app/

## Download

- **Mac** (macOS 14+, Apple Silicon): [ClipSync-mac.zip](https://github.com/cyberwizard-dev/clipsync-app/releases/latest/download/ClipSync-mac.zip)
  The app isn't notarized yet: unzip it, move it to Applications, then right-click → Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).
- **Windows** (10/11, 64-bit): [ClipSync-windows-x64.zip](https://github.com/cyberwizard-dev/clipsync-app/releases/latest/download/ClipSync-windows-x64.zip)
  Unzip and run ClipSync.exe. It isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: More info → Run anyway.
- **Android** (8+): [ClipSync.apk](https://github.com/cyberwizard-dev/clipsync-app/releases/latest/download/ClipSync.apk)
  Allow your browser to install apps. On first launch the app walks you through auto-send setup with Wireless debugging (Android 11+, no computer needed).

## Licensing

Every new pairing gets a 7-day free trial on up to 2 devices. After that, buy a license on the [website](https://cyberwizard-dev.github.io/clipsync-app/#pricing) or in the app: 3 or 5 devices for 3 months, 6 months or 1 year (from $4.99), charged in naira at today's rate. Extra devices are $3.99 each.
